# Code audit, 19 Oct 2026: duplicates and gaps

Read-only audit of the whole codebase. Nothing was changed. Items are ranked within each section; "verified" means a
person re-read the code, the rest came from a systematic sweep and should be re-read before fixing.

## 1. Permission and scoping gaps

| # | Severity | Where | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HIGH (verified) | `UserAccessController::storeRole/destroyRole/storeOverride` | Anyone with `access.manage` can give themselves (or anyone) the Owner role, remove Owner from another owner, or add deny overrides to an owner. `RoleController::people` and `UserController::update` already block this. | `abort_if` when the target role is Owner or the target user is an owner and the actor is not an owner. |
| 2 | MED-HIGH (verified) | `POST /packaging/orders/{order}/hold` → `ScanService::hold` | No check that the caller is the order's packer or that the order is in the packaging group. Source `scan` skips the state machine permission and booking-claim lock, so any packer can hold a New or Confirmed order by ID, even mid-booking. | Require `packer_id === by` (or `packaging.manage`) and a packaging-group status. |
| 3 | MED | `POST /customers/{c}/fraud-check` (`customers.view`), `GET /customers/steadfast?phone=` | Paid Steadfast lookups with no throttle; `force: true` bypasses cache. | `throttle:10,1` + per-customer cooldown; POST under `customers.edit`. |
| 4 | MED | `HoldLimitController@decide`, `OrderService::decideAdvanceWaiver` | A manager can approve their own hold-limit request; a waiver can be decided when none was asked. Points and Scorecard already block self-approval. | Same `abort_if(self)`; require a pending request. |
| 5 | MED | `GET /packaging/orders/{order}` JSON, `/packaging/sheet?run=` | Skip the pause filter, so packers can see paused orders by ID. | Apply `PackagingPause::visibleSql` unless `canControl`. |
| 6 | LOW-MED | `GET /shipping/labels?orders=`, `GET /packaging/labels?orders=` | GET under a view permission writes `printed_at` / issues labels. Shipping one has no cap. | Make POST or move under `*.create`; cap at 100. |
| 7 | LOW-MED | `/kpi`, `/upsell` | No date-range cap; builds the whole team then filters to one row. | Clamp to 92 days; build only the caller's row without `kpi.view`. |
| 8 | LOW | `CustomerCallController@incoming` | Creates a Customer without `customers.create`. | Gate the create. |
| 9 | LOW | `GET /issues` | Shows every issue (name, COD) to own-scope users. Documented as intended. | Consider masking name/COD for own-scope. |
| 10 | LOW | `TelegramController` picked / stock-issue callbacks | Any linked staff member can act, no `packaging.create` check. | Add the check. |
| 11 | LOW | `WooCommerceWebhookController` ping | Unauthenticated POST with `webhook_id` sets the "connected" flag (no DB write). | Require the signature for the ping too. |
| 12 | LOW | `POST /orders/{order}/website-status` | Not scoped to the caller's visible orders. | Reuse `authorizeWork`. |

Done right (trusted): all three webhooks verify a secret/signature with idempotent raw-payload storage and throttling;
`authorizeWork` covers every order mutation; payments/COD/refunds check `payments.verify`/`orders.approve`; call lists,
points, upsell, KPI and exports are owner-scoped on the server; pack/unpack/pause/resync are guarded; request inputs
(`show`, `status`, dates, `orders`, `run`) are whitelisted or cast; no user array reaches `fillable`/`forceFill`.

## 2. Same number, different formulas (reports that will disagree)
